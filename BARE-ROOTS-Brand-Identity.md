*Prepared by Couture House Co. · Richmond, VA*

## 1. The Brand in One Line

**Bare Roots is Richmond's luxury natural-hair house where holistic hair recovery meets master loc artistry — rooted in Black culture, crowned in gold.**

---

## 2. The Origin Story (The "Why")

Bare Roots is not a startup — it's a *merger of mastery*. Two women, already sharing one studio at **8030 W Broad St #117**, are joining their crafts under a single crown:

- **Iisha** — *Holistic Hair Recovery Practitioner & Educator.* Brooklyn-born, Richmond-rooted. She grows hair "from the inside out," treating thinning, alopecia and scalp health through wellness, science and soul. Founder of the forthcoming **DOPE HAIR** accessory line.
- **Sabrina Sutton** — *Creative Loc Artist & Educator.* A meticulous artist of micro locs, sisterlocs, traditional locs, color and silk press. Premium custom hair wraps are next.

Where most salons sell a *style*, Bare Roots sells a *root system*: heal it, grow it, adorn it, maintain it, and teach the client to own it. Recovery + Artistry + Education + Product, under one luxurious roof.

**Brand promise:** *We don't just do your hair. We return you to your roots — and crown them.*

## 4. Brand Personality & Voice

**Personality:** Rooted · Regal · Warm · Knowledgeable · Culturally proud · Calm-luxury (a spa, not a hype beast).

**Voice principles**
1. **Speak to the crown, not the flaw.** ("Your regrowth starts with one conversation" — not "fix your bald spots.")
2. **Luxury is calm.** Short, confident, unhurried sentences. Never salesy-loud.
3. **Culture is the foundation, not a costume.** Language is proud and familiar without caricature.
4. **Educator energy.** We explain the *why* — clients leave smarter.

**On-brand words:** rooted, crown, ritual, restore, grow, nourish, artistry, house, from the inside out, adorn.
**Avoid:** cheap, deal, quick, basic, "bald," clinical coldness.

**Taglines (approved set)**
- *Rooted in culture. Crowned in gold.* (primary)
- *Natural. Luxury. Culture.* (descriptor lock-up)
- *Grow from the root.*
- *Where the crown is cared for.*

## 6. Typography

| Use | Typeface | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Display / Headlines** | **Cormorant Garamond** (500/600, italic for accents) | High-contrast serif = editorial luxury. Big, confident, airy. |
| **Body / UI** | **Jost** (300–600) | Clean geometric sans; let-spaced small caps for labels. |
| **Eyebrows / labels** | Jost, 11px, `letter-spacing: .3em`, UPPERCASE | The "couture" detail line above headings. |

*Production alternates if licensing needed:* Playfair Display / Fraunces (display), Montserrat / Poppins (body).

**Type behaviors**
- Italic serif for emotional lines ("crowned in *gold*").
- Prices set in italic serif gold — turns a price into a jewel.
- Generous line-height and whitespace. Luxury breathes.

## 7. Motion & "Natural" Design Language

The brand's signature feel = **gold that drifts like pollen, and roots that grow.**

- **Gold particle drift** in the hero (slow, upward, sparse) — the "gold in the air."
- **Reveal-on-scroll**: content rises 40px + fades (GSAP ScrollTrigger). Calm, never bouncy.
- **Root/bloom SVG motifs**: locs flowing into roots; small gold blossoms as section accents.
- **Hover**: cards lift 6–8px, a thin gold bar grows down their left edge, "+" buttons rotate.
- **Marquee ribbon** of services in italic serif — an editorial runway strip.
- **Botanical accents** (leaf/flower line-art in gold) mark section breaks = the "Natural / flower-like" cue.

*Production upgrades:* Three.js gold-dust field or floating petals in hero; Framer Motion page transitions; frame-by-frame scroll storytelling for the Root Recovery Method™; Higgsfield video B-roll of locs & gold light.

## 8. Logo System

Three directions generated in Higgsfield (see delivered image set). Recommended usage once you pick:

1. **Root / Tree-of-Life emblem** — locs → roots in gold on forest green. *Leans hardest into "Natural / Roots."* Best as the primary seal.
2. **BR Monogram crest** — interlocking B+R in gold, ringed, on plum. *Most fashion-house.* Best for favicon, stamps, embroidery, wax-seal moments.
3. **Wordmark + gold bloom** — "BARE ROOTS" serif with a single gold flower. *Cleanest / most versatile.* Best for site header, signage, print.

**Recommended architecture:** Use the **Wordmark** as the everyday logo, the **Monogram** as the icon/favicon/adornment mark, and the **Tree-of-Life emblem** as the ceremonial seal (loyalty, packaging, certificates). One family, three registers.

**Clear space & don'ts:** keep clear space = height of the "B"; never place gold logo on busy photos without a scrim; never recolor gold to yellow; never stretch.

---

## 11. Brand Do / Don't

**Do:** breathe (whitespace), let gold be rare, lead with the crown, explain the science, honor culture, photograph real texture and real regrowth.
**Don't:** clutter, over-gild, use stocky "corporate" imagery, shame the client's hair loss, rush the luxury.
